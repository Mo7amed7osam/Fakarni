What Fakarni does

Fakarni is a voice-first reminder app. Users can speak a reminder, review the parsed result, save it, and receive local notifications. The app may also optionally save reminders to the device calendar or linked calendar providers, depending on user settings.

Data the app may process

The app may process reminder content, reminder schedule data, optional calendar data required to create calendar events, optional anonymous analytics events, and optional voice transcript data during smart parsing. Reminder data is primarily stored locally on the device.

Permissions

Fakarni may request access to the microphone, speech recognition, notifications, and calendar. These permissions are used only for the related app features and are not required all at once.

Remote services

If smart parsing is enabled in production, reminder text may be sent to the configured parsing gateway in order to improve reminder interpretation.
