Common questions

How do I create a reminder?
Open Fakarni, tap the main microphone button, and say the task and time naturally.

Do I have to type everything manually?
No. Fakarni is designed as a voice-first reminder flow. Manual review is only used when the result needs correction.

Why does the app ask for microphone or speech permission?
Those permissions are required only when using voice capture features.

Why does the app ask for calendar access?
Calendar access is optional and only needed if the user wants reminders saved to the calendar.

Troubleshooting

If voice capture is not working, confirm microphone and speech recognition permissions are enabled. If reminders are not arriving on time, confirm local notifications are allowed in iOS settings.
